## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end South Asian bridal opulence translated into modern editorial minimalism. The visual language blends royal heritage architecture (jali motifs, arched symmetry, polished marble) with the restrained layout architecture of haute couture lookbooks and museum monographs.

### Target Audience & Emotional Palette
- **Audience:** Discerning couples, luxury wedding producers, high-net-worth event stylists, and bespoke floral and stage designers seeking heirloom-grade inventory (handcrafted mandaps, bespoke brassware, royal seating, antique textiles).
- **Tone & Mood:** Grandeur without clutter, majestic reverence, architectural serenity, and pristine curation. Every screen must feel tactile, deliberate, and quiet—allowing opulent gold-leaf furniture and intricate embroidery to command attention.

### Design Movement
**Minimalist Editorial Luxury:**
- Expansive, breathing negative space framed by subtle hairline structures.
- Intentional asymmetry paired with classical axial alignment.
- Muted canvas textures accented by metallic champagne foil reflections, avoiding ostentatious or saturated yellow golds in favor of aged, muted brass and warm champagne hues.

## Colors

The palette revolves around historical Indian royal pigments rendered in contemporary tones: aged champagne leaf, unbleached silk parchment, warm raw linen, and deep charred espresso.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C5A059`):** Soft warm champagne gold used for focal CTAs, subtle highlight borders, key badges, and select typographic moments.
- **Secondary (`#1A1816`):** Deep rich espresso black, grounding the design with maximum legibility while avoiding the clinical harshness of `#000000`.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6D37`):** Antique burnished bronze, used for hover states, focused interaction rings, and secondary iconography.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm alabaster/ivory foundation that provides an organic, tactile canvas.

### Surface Tonal Mapping
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8F5` (Ivory Silk)
- **Layered Surface / Container:** `#F5F0EA` (Warm Alabaster)
- **Elevated Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pristine Crisp White, creating subtle crisp contrast against the ivory background)
- **Subtle Hairline Borders:** `#EAE3D9` (Warm Sandstone)
- **Gold Accent Border / Inset:** `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`
- **Body Text:** `#2B2623` (Softened Charred Umber)
- **Muted Subtext / Captions:** `#6B635B` (Aged Slate Ochre)

## Typography

The type system creates tension between the lyrical, regal curves of **Playfair Display** and the geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

### Typographic Principles
- **Editorial Titles:** Headlines must lean into classical proportions with generous vertical breathing room. Titles frequently adopt title case or soft sentence case with italic accents for emotional emphasis (e.g., *The Mandap Collection*).
- **Ultra-Tracked Overlines:** Category tags, item specifications, and micro-headers use `label-caps` set in all-caps with extreme letter tracking (`0.22em`) to echo high-jewelry and auction catalog conventions.
- **Lightweight Longform:** Body copy uses a lighter weight (`300` or `400`) with generous line-heights (`1.6x` to `1.8x`) to evoke calm and unhurried reading.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a strictly measured, museum-grade 12-column grid. Generous margins and deep vertical cadences ensure every furniture piece, candelabra, or archway feels presented as a one-of-a-kind art object.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `4rem` (64px) outer margin, `2rem` (32px) gutter. Max container width: 1440px centered.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `2.5rem` (40px) outer margin, `1.5rem` (24px) gutter.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column grid, `1.5rem` (24px) outer margin, `1rem` (16px) gutter.

### Composition Rules
- **Vertical Spacing Hierarchy:** Major exhibition tiers and rental suite collections separate via `space-xl` multiplied by 1.5 (`6rem`), avoiding compressed vertical density.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Lookbook layouts frequently pair 7-column hero imagery with 5-column offset detail descriptors to honor editorial magazine pagination.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain a tactile luxury feel, visual hierarchy relies on subtle, diffuse ambient lighting rather than heavy digital dropshadows.

### Depth Paradigms
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth is primarily defined through single-pixel borders (`#EAE3D9`) paired with internal pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces layered above the `#FAF8F5` ivory canvas.
- **Warm Ambient Shadow:** Shadows are tinted with espresso and warm ochre, eliminating cool neutral grays:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 38, 35, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(43, 38, 35, 0.02)`
  - *Interactive Hover Elevation:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 38, 35, 0.03)`
  - *Floating Booking Drawer / Modal:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 24, 22, 0.14)`
- **Gold Leaf Accents:** High-tier interaction points (active dates, selected inventory tags) use a subtle interior foil effect: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 0 (Sharp)** corner geometry.

### Architectural Rationale
- Inspired by hand-cut sandstone, monolithic marble plinths, and bespoke stationery, crisp right angles project timeless prestige, formality, and architectural permanence.
- **Micro-Elements:** Inputs, cards, modal sheets, and buttons feature crisp 0px radii.
- **Architectural Motifs (Curved Exceptions):** Selective structural elements (such as hero image frames or feature vignettes) may utilize an authentic, bespoke Mughal/scalloped arch curve along the top edge while maintaining sharp 90-degree corner boundaries at the base.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (The Gold Plinth):** Solid `#C5A059` background with `#1A1816` text. Sharp corners, letter-spaced uppercase text (`label-caps`). Subtle scale-down hover with smooth shift to `#B89047`.
- **Secondary Action (Charcoal Regal):** Solid `#1A1816` background with `#FAF8F5` text. Used for "Reserve Consultation" or "Confirm Curation".
- **Ghost Editorial Button:** Transparent background, single-pixel hairline border in `#1A1816` or `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.8)`, accompanied by an elongated right directional arrow (`→`). On hover, background fills with `#FAF8F5` or champagne tint.

### Rental Product Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface resting on the `#FAF8F5` background, bordered by a 1px `#EAE3D9` hairline frame.
- **Image Presentation:** 4:5 vertical portrait aspect ratio to emphasize tall mandap pillars, thrones, and brass urns. Includes subtle zoom transitions (1.03x) on hover.
- **Card Content:** Category overline in `label-caps` (`#8C6D37`), product title in `Playfair Display`, followed by rental pricing format: *"Curated from $1,200 / Day"*.
- **Quick Inquire Floating Action:** Appears on image hover as a minimal bottom-pinned bar with 0px border radius.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- **Underlined Field Style:** Minimalist bottom-border-only input fields (`1px solid #EAE3D9`), transitioning to `1.5px solid #C5A059` on focus.
- **Floating Labels:** Micro-labels set in `label-caps` in `#6B635B`, maintaining absolute tracking.
- **Event Date Picker:** Custom dual-calendar view displaying auspicious date markers, enveloped in an ivory frame with champagne hover highlights.

### Chips & Filters (Event Type / Material)
- **Resting:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#EAE3D9`, text `#2B2623` in `body-sm`.
- **Selected State:** `#1A1816` background, `#FAF8F5` text with a delicate champagne accent dot, conveying curated selection without pill shapes.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp square (0px radius) with 1px border `#C5A059`. When checked, solid `#1A1816` fill with an antique gold checkmark.
- **Radios:** Concentric sharp diamond or circular hairline rings with a solid `#C5A059` center dot.

### Specialized Component: Moodboard & Wishlist Drawer
- Side-sliding drawer utilizing `#FAF8F5` backdrop, separated from the canvas by a vertical gold hairline (`1px solid rgba(197,160,89,0.3)`).
- Items display thumbnail, tier count, dimension specs, and an "Availability for Event Date" indicator styled as a micro-dot in muted emerald or warm gold.
